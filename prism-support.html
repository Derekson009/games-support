Prism — Support

How do I play? A lamp fires a beam of light across the board. Tap a mirror to turn it and bounce the beam around corners. A board is complete when every crystal on it is lit at the same time.

What do prisms do? A prism (the triangle) splits the beam three ways: straight through, left, and right. Some boards can only be finished by splitting the light.

What stops the beam? Dark stone blocks and the edge of the board. The beam passes through crystals and prisms.

Stuck? Every board is solvable — the answer is never more than a few mirror turns away. "Reset mirrors" puts the board back to its starting position.

Progress missing? Your completed boards are saved on your device; deleting the app clears them.
