Air Hockey — Privacy Policy

Air Hockey does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Your unlocked rivals, matches won, career goals for and against, and your sound setting are stored only on your own device and are removed if you delete the app.

If you contact us for support, we will only use your email address to reply.
