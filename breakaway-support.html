Breakaway — Support

How do I play? A matchup is a classic shootout: five shots each way. First you shoot — the goalie shows a stance, and the caption names his tell. Pick the shot his stance can't cover: Top Shelf, Deke Left, Deke Right, or Five-Hole. Then you play goalie: the shooter tips his hand in his wind-up, and you answer with Stand Tall, Butterfly, Slide Left, or Slide Right. Score more than the goalie over five rounds to win.

What do the goalie's tells mean? Drops Early leaves the top of the net open (go Top Shelf) and covers the Five-Hole. Stays Tall is the reverse — the Five-Hole is open and the top is covered. Cheats Glove Side leans one way and can't get back across, so deke to the opposite side; Cheats Blocker Side mirrors it. Shots a stance doesn't favor or cover land in between.

What if it's tied after five rounds? Sudden death: you keep trading shot pairs, and the first pair that ends with someone ahead decides the matchup.

Who are the goalies? A ladder of five: Rookie Roy, Steady Eddie, The Wall, The Old Pro, and The Champion. The reads never change — higher goalies just punish a right read less and show subtler stances from The Wall up. Each goalie's scouting report is shown before the matchup.

How do goalies unlock? Beat a goalie to unlock the next one up the ladder. Any goalie you've unlocked can be replayed from the ladder on the menu.

Progress missing? Your unlocked goalies, career goals and saves, and matchups won are saved on your device; deleting the app clears them.
