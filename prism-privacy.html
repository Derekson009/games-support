Prism — Privacy Policy

Prism does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Your completed boards and sound setting are stored only on your own device and are removed if you delete the app.

If you contact us for support, we will only use your email address to reply.
