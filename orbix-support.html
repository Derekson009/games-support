Orbix — Support

How do I play? Tap a piece in the tray to select it, use Rotate and Flip to turn it, then tap the board to place it. A level is solved when every piece is placed and every cell of the board is filled — no overlaps, no gaps.

How do I move a piece I've already placed? Tap it on the board to lift it back to the tray, then place it again somewhere else.

Circles or Squares? The chips at the top of the screen (and on the start screen) switch how the pieces are drawn. It is the same puzzle either way — pick the look you like.

Stuck? Every level is solvable — all 12 were verified by a solver before release. Try lifting pieces back and matching the awkward corner to the piece shaped like it. Reset puts the level back to its starting position.

Progress missing? Your unlocked levels and any board in progress are saved on your device; deleting the app clears them.
