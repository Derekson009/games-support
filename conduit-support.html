Conduit — Support

Stuck on a level? Every level in Conduit is verified solvable. Work backwards from the drain: trace which pipe openings have to meet, one tile at a time. Tap a tile to rotate it a quarter-turn clockwise.

Flow says blocked? Water only flows where pipe openings actually meet. Follow your path joint by joint — a pipe that points at a wall or at the closed side of its neighbour stops the flow there.

Progress missing? Progress is saved on your device; deleting the app clears it.
