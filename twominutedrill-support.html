Two-Minute Drill — Support

How do I play? You're the quarterback, down 4, with two minutes to drive the field. Each snap, the defense shows its look — read the formation diagram and the plain-English tell, then call the play that beats it: Run, Short Pass, Deep Shot, or Screen.

How does the clock work? Just like the real thing: completions and runs keep the clock moving, incompletions stop it, and each play costs a few seconds. You get 2 timeouts per drive — use one and the next play costs almost no time.

What beats what? A stacked box begs for a deep shot. A blitz gets burned by a screen or a quick throw. Against prevent coverage, take the easy yards underneath — the deep ball is a trap. The commentary after each play tells you why it worked or didn't.

What are skins? Skins change who acts out each play on the field — classic players, or Santa and the reindeer. Pick one on the start screen; it's just for fun and changes nothing about the game.

Progress missing? Your best drive streak and touchdown totals are saved on your device; deleting the app clears them.
