Flux — Support

Stuck in a bay? Every bay in Flux is verified solvable. Work backwards from the exit: find what's blocking the cyan shuttle, then find what blocks that. Undo lets you back up one slide at a time, and Reset starts the bay fresh.

A car won't slide? Vehicles only slide along their own direction — cars and buses move the way they face. Drag from the middle of the vehicle and pull past the next square.

Progress missing? Progress is saved on your device; deleting the app clears it.
