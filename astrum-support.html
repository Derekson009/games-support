Astrum — Support

How do I play? Tap a gem, then tap an adjacent gem to swap them. If the swap lines up three or more relics of the same type in a row or column, they clear, new gems fall from above, and chain reactions multiply your score. A swap that makes no match simply swaps back.

What are the three modes? Endless is the open game — play as long as moves remain and chase your best score. Daily Board is one board seeded by the date, the same for everyone that day, with your result saved on your device. Target Mode is a ladder of rounds: reach the round's target score within its move limit to advance; rounds get steeper as you climb, and shuffle is disabled in Target so a dead board can fail the round.

What does Shuffle do? In Endless and Daily, Shuffle deals you a fresh board — guaranteed to have no instant matches and at least one valid move — while keeping your score. It is free and unlimited. There is no Shuffle in Target Mode.

What are Goals? Milestones you collect as you play — big chains, long runs, high scores, and more. There are 14 to earn, each one celebrates the first time you reach it, and the Goals screen keeps track of your collection.

Does my game save? Yes. A game in progress in each mode is saved on your device as you play, so you can leave and pick it back up with Continue. Your best score, daily results, Target progress, and goals are saved on your device too; deleting the app clears them.

Is there a timer? No timer and no lives in Endless and Daily — play at your own pace. Only Target Mode limits you, and only by moves, never by the clock.
