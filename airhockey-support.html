Air Hockey — Support

How do I play? Hold your phone sideways — Air Hockey is played on a wide landscape table. You defend the goal on the left. Drag your mallet anywhere on your half to move it, and strike the puck into the rival's goal on the right. A moving mallet hits harder, so swing through the puck for a smash. First to 7 goals wins the match.

Do own goals count? Yes — a puck that crosses your own goal line counts for the rival, so wild swings near your net are risky.

Who are the rivals? A ladder of five, each with his own style: Camper Cam guards his goal, Chaser Chase attacks every puck, Bank-Shot Banksy plays the angles off the walls, Dasher Dana is fast all over the table, and Champion Gordie defends the line and counterattacks. Each rival's scouting report is shown before the match.

How do rivals unlock? Beat a rival to unlock the next one up the ladder. Any rival you've unlocked can be replayed from the ladder on the menu.

The puck stopped moving. If the puck dies where nobody can reach it, it is re-served at center ice after a couple of seconds.

Progress missing? Your unlocked rivals, matches won, and career goals are saved on your device; deleting the app clears them.
