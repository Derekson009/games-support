Breakaway — Privacy Policy

Breakaway does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Your unlocked goalies, goalies beaten, career goals and saves, matchups won, and your sound setting are stored only on your own device and are removed if you delete the app.

If you contact us for support, we will only use your email address to reply.
