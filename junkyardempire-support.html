Junkyard Empire — Support

How do I sort? Drag an item onto its matching material bin, or tap an item to select it, then tap its bin. The seven bins are Wood, Copper, Aluminum, Appliances, E-Waste, Furniture, and Landfill.

What is contamination? Dropping an item in the wrong bin costs $2 and 1 reputation, and breaks your streak. Five clean sorts in a row earn a $10 streak bonus.

What do upgrades do? Open the wrench shop to buy gear with your earnings: gloves and scanners raise item values, the conveyor brings bigger truckloads, and the sorting robot clears 3 items from every load for you.

Progress missing? Your cash, reputation, and upgrades are saved on your device; deleting the app clears them. You can also reset the yard from the shop.
