Fletch — Support

How do I play? Every cell on the board holds an arrow. Tap an arrow and, if the lane in the direction it points is completely clear all the way to the edge, it flies off the board. Clear every arrow to finish the level.

What happens when I tap a blocked arrow? Nothing flies — that counts as a miss, and misses cost stars. A level can always be finished; clean play with no misses earns all three stars.

Stuck? Every level is solvable — all 32 were verified by a solver before release, and each board is built so a clearing order always exists. Use Hint to pulse an arrow that can fly right now, Undo to take back your last move, or Reset to start the board over. If no arrow can move, the game tells you — undo or reset and try a different order.

What is the Daily? A fresh 7×7 board seeded by the date — the same board for everyone that day. Your daily results are saved on your device.

How do levels unlock? Clearing a level unlocks the next one. Boards grow from 3×3 up to 10×10 across the 32 levels.

Progress missing? Your cleared levels, stars, and daily results are saved on your device; deleting the app clears them.
