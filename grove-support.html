Grove — Support

How do I merge? Drag any plant onto an identical one and they become the next in the chain: Seed → Sprout → Leaf → Bud → Bloom → Fruit → Grove → Elder. Merges work from any distance, and you can drag plants onto empty squares to rearrange the garden.

What do two Elders do? Elders can't merge upward — instead, drag one Elder onto another to return them to the grove for a 2,560-point bonus and free up space.

What is Daily Grove? A fresh garden seeded by the date, the same for every player that day. It has its own saved board and its own best score, separate from your regular garden.

Progress missing? Your garden, goals, and best scores are saved on your device; deleting the app clears them.
