Astrum — Privacy Policy

Astrum does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Your games in progress (in Endless, Daily, and Target modes), your daily results, your Target round progress, the goals you have earned, your best score, and your sound setting are stored only on your own device and are removed if you delete the app.

If you contact us for support, we will only use your email address to reply.
