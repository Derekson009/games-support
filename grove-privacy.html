Grove — Privacy Policy

Grove does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Your game progress, goals, best scores, and Daily Grove results are stored only on your own device and are removed if you delete the app.

If you contact us for support, we will only use your email address to reply.
