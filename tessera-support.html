Tessera — Support

How do I play? Tap a piece in the tray to select it, then tap the board to place it. Fill every cell in a row or a column and that line clears, scoring bonus points. You get a fresh deal of three pieces whenever the tray runs out.

How does a run end? The run ends when none of your remaining pieces fit anywhere on the board. Clearing lines is how you keep space open — a single line is worth far more than the pieces that made it.

Placed a piece in the wrong spot? Undo takes back your last placement, exactly as it was. It works once per move, so place with care after that.

What are goals? Milestones for clears, scores, and pieces placed. Open the Goals panel to see what you have earned and what is still locked.

Progress missing? Your current run, best score, and goals are saved on your device; deleting the app clears them.
