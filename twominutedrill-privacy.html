Two-Minute Drill — Privacy Policy

Two-Minute Drill does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Your best streak, touchdown totals, sound setting, and chosen skin are stored only on your own device and are removed if you delete the app.

If you contact us for support, we will only use your email address to reply.
